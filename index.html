01 — About

Between the

I'm Besho Medhat — a production coordinator and assistant director based in Cairo, working across independent and festival-screened fiction film and TV. My work sits between meticulous planning and the reality of a live set: schedules, budgets, departments, and people, all kept moving toward the same story.

I bring organization and follow-through to every production I join, backed by a background in computer science that sharpens how I research, build presentations, and use AI tools for production admin and pitch decks — practical support that keeps a production office running smoothly.
